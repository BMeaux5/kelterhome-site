The Name

Where Kelter comes from.

The name Kelter has been in the family since 1828, born from a gathering of involved citizens who believed that community, order, and shared prosperity mattered. We're still building on that.

Who We Are

Two people. One home. A lot of experience.

Brandon Morgan spent 20 years inside manufacturing plants, healthcare systems, and PE-backed companies figuring out why things break and how to fix them for good. He's an engineer by training - Georgia Tech, then an MBA from LSU - and a systems thinker by nature. What he kept seeing, at work and at home, was that chaos isn't inevitable. It's just what happens when there's no system.

Mikeila Morgan has been organizing things for as long as she can remember. What began as a childhood instinct to create order and structure eventually became a professional strength. Over the course of her career in planning and project management, she developed a talent for coordinating complex projects, organizing information, and creating systems that bring clarity to challenging situations. Her approach is equal parts analytical and compassionate. She understands that every organizing project is unique, and she works alongside clients to create spaces that are functional, sustainable, and supportive of their goals. Mikeila believes that organization is about more than having a tidy home - it is about creating calm, reducing stress, and making everyday life a little easier.

Together we bring process and instinct. Structure and soul. The operational framework and the belief that a home should feel like somewhere you want to be.

What We Have Lived

We built this from the chaos we survived.

Between us, we have bought and sold multiple homes, raised children, lost parents to cancer, navigated divorce, remarried, dissolved estates, divided land, prepared for hurricanes, and built a life in the mountains of Western North Carolina. We know what it feels like when life gets hard and the home still has to run.

We also know what it feels like when it doesn't.

You can't go over it. You can't go under it. You have to go through it. The hard things in life are like that. You just have to go through them.

Kelter Home is what we built from going through it. Not a theory. Not a methodology we read in a book. A real approach to running a home, built from experience we didn't ask for and systems we built to survive it.

We believe a well-run home changes everything. And we believe you can get there, whatever that looks like for your family.
