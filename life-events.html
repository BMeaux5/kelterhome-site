Program 02

Life Events

Life doesn't pause for the home to catch up. When everything changes, you need a plan. We've built one for ten of life's most disruptive transitions.

The Approach

Every life event is a system under pressure.

Think of it like disaster preparedness. When a hurricane is coming, you don't figure out the plan as it hits. You prepare. You have a checklist. You know what to do first, second, and third.

Life events work the same way. A new baby, a death in the family, a major move — each one has a window to prepare, a set of things that need to happen, and a defined end state. We help you get there with less chaos and more clarity.

Between us, we have lived through nearly every one of these. We built these guides from that experience.

The Ten Events

Where we can help.

Each event has its own protocol — a structured approach to navigating the transition without losing the home in the process.

Marriage
Combining two households, two systems, two sets of habits. How to build one home that actually works for both of you.
Pregnancy and New Child
Nine months to prepare the home for a person who will change everything about how it runs. What to do, when to do it, and what not to waste money on.
Major Move and Home Purchase
One of the most logistically complex things a family does. Buying, selling, moving, settling in — with a system for each phase.
Divorce and Separation
Dividing a shared home and life into two functioning households. What needs to happen, in what order, without making it harder than it already is.
Empty Nest
The home that was built around children now needs to work for two. A transition most people underestimate until they're in the middle of it.
Aging Parent
Whether they move in or need more support from a distance, integrating an aging parent into the family system requires planning most families don't do in advance.
Death and Estate
What happens to the home, the things, and the administration of a life when someone is gone. We have been through this. We know what needs to happen and what can wait.
Retirement and Downsizing
The home that worked for one season of life may not work for the next. Rightsizing, simplifying, and building a home that supports what comes next.
Medical Crisis
When illness hits, the home has to keep running. Practical support for families navigating the operational demands of a serious diagnosis.
Disaster Prep and Recovery
From hurricane preparation to post-disaster recovery, a protocol built from real experience. Know what to do before, during, and after.
